# Research

## Surface research

- **Scope**: Determine whether "right proactivity" is a defensible extreme axis for Arden, and what
  its concrete design must be. Six independent threads, fanned out to subagents on 2026-08-09:

  | Thread | Question | Worker |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | T1 Arden audit | What is proactive in Arden today; is it push or pull; what does a wrong guess cost the user | `arden-audit` (repo, read-only) |
  | T2 Energy moat | What getenergy.com actually is, its thesis, and what it structurally cannot do | `energy-research` (web) |
  | T3 ChatGPT Pulse | Mechanism, rollout limits, stated reasons for gating, real reception; why proactive features die | `pulse-research` (web) |
  | T4 OpenAI roles | 2026 "personal AGI"/proactive postings and leadership statements; what they think the hard problem is | `openai-roles` (web) |
  | T5 ADHD demand | Why productivity tooling fails ADHD users, what they ask for, whether notifications cut through | `adhd-demand` (web/Reddit) |
  | T6 User demand | What users ask for and refuse from proactive agents; trust boundary, local-first, inspectability, RSI | `user-demand` (web/Reddit/HN) |

- **Sources inspected**: pending worker returns; local spot-check of `apps/server/arden/areas/` (see below).
- **Observations (pre-fan-out spot check, 2026-08-09)**:
  - The area custodian's per-run output is a list of nominated asks; `record_area_run` filters by
    `SALIENCE_THRESHOLD` and truncates to `MAX_ASKS_PER_RUN`
    (`apps/server/arden/areas/agent.py:284-313`).
  - Ask kinds are `notify | question | review` (`apps/server/arden/areas/models.py:7`); `notify` is
    defined as "FYI, no decision (Home only, expires quietly)" (`models.py:4`) and receives a TTL
    (`agent.py:293`).
  - Non-agent ask sources exist too: pending approvals become `review` asks and failed automation runs
    become `notify` asks (`apps/server/arden/areas/service.py:60-96`).
- **Negative evidence**: no ledger predating this task
  (`rg -l '<!-- development-ledger:v(1|2) -->' .` returned nothing, 2026-08-09).

## Conflicts and gaps

- **Resolved**: the custodian prompt *forces* an ask on stall (F-03), though zero-ask runs are permitted in general (F-01).
- **Tooling gap — reddit.com is blocked** for the subagents' web tools (rejected by allowlist), so T5's Reddit
  evidence is entirely secondary (aggregators, an arXiv survey, practitioner blogs). The user explicitly asked
  for Reddit. Primary Reddit threads still need collecting via the browser pane. T6 may hit the same wall.
- **Tooling gap — x.com returns HTTP 402** to the fetch tool, so Energy's launch thread, its thesis wording,
  and the hiring-ask criteria are unverified (F-17).
- Conflict to watch: F-19/F-11 favour batched, quiet, deliverable-bearing surfacing; F-10 says user-*directed*
  beat model-*inferred*. A purely inferred ambient page may fail for the same reason Pulse did, even without
  interrupting. Ambient is necessary but not sufficient.
- Unresolved and user-blocking: which surface the user actually experiences as "a lot of notifications",
  given no OS push exists (F-05).
