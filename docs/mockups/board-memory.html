marathon training

Coach notes from Ana Ferreira: keep long-run pace 60–75 seconds slower than goal pace, and stop banking miles in any week where sleep drops under 7 hours. Fueling every 35 minutes on anything over 90 minutes.

Volume is climbing toward 55 km against the health sleep rule — recovery weeks matter more now than added distance. Gear is settled; the softer trail section spared the knee on the 24 km long run, so long runs stay on trail until taper.

Next block: hold 55 km for two weeks, then the first 30 km dress rehearsal on the race route — gels every 35 minutes, bottle drop at the turn, easy days capped at 6:30/km.
