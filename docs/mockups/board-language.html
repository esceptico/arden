Desk & Paper — the consolidated language

One sentence: the content is the room; the interface is furniture that floats above it. The base plane of every surface is a reading room — flat, quiet, edge-to-edge. Everything operational is desk material floating over it in a strict stack, with depth from the app's existing shadow ladder, recede, and motion — never glass. Each claim below is rendered with the real tokens.

01

Two materials

everything is one of these — no third material

Paper is what you read and produce; it IS the window: white, radius 0, hairline rules. Desk is what you operate: warm-tinted panels that float. The only warmth in the system lives in desk fills — the text ramp is identical on both.

03

Motion

approved — spring in, tween out · press the buttons

Layers enter on springs (peek: 210/26, ~450ms settle) and leave on crisp tweens (160ms). Content rises through small blur (RISE_IN: y 6→0, blur 3→0, 30ms stagger); layer slides stay sharp. The native shell is invariant: traffic lights remain fixed at x18 / y18; the global sidebar toggle remains fixed at x84 / y14. The sidebar is furniture, not window chrome: showing slides it in for 300ms, hiding dissolves it with a 48px left drift and 6px blur for 400ms, and the workspace reclaims the same lane concurrently. ⌘B always preserves this motion. Axis follows the controller: horizontally ordered tabs swap their content plane by 8px with 2px softening on the same 210/26 entrance spring as the peek; vertical RISE_IN is reserved for row arrival and disclosure. Keyboard commands that open or close spatial layers preserve their motion: ⌘S raises the review sheet through a shorter 24px, snappier 360/32 · mass .75 spring, while Escape lowers the sheet or dismisses the peek. Inline wiki links use a neutral version of the peek spring. Peek names roll like an odometer: the old name exits in 160ms and the new name enters vertically on the 210/26 spring with 1.4px motion blur sharpening over 260ms; the roll runs only when the peek object changes. Type has exactly six roles: display 24, section 18, reading 15/1.65, body 13/1.55, control 12/500, and metadata 10.5 Mono. Action labels—including peek names and relationship tabs—use Geist, never Mono; Mono is reserved for counts, dates, breadcrumbs, status, and diffs. Directory and file rows are both 13px; hierarchy comes from indentation, chevrons, color, and weight—not type size. The desktop composition has one center: the sidebar defaults to 288px, resizes from 220–400px, and owns a 32px separation lane. The remaining workspace centers a 760px page shell with 28px inner gutters, producing a 704px reading measure. Hiding or resizing the sidebar changes the available room; it never changes the page’s maximum width or creates a second alignment axis. Sidebar modes preserve identity: every mode keeps its icon. The active mode expands to icon + label while inactive labels move out: [icon + label] [icon] [icon]. Selection transfers horizontally in 220ms through one shared layout surface; the active tab takes its content width while the remaining icon tabs divide the available rail. The pill is therefore ordinary layout—icon, 6px gap, label, and 10px end padding—not glyph measurement or hand-tuned offsets. Labels cross a restrained 2px blur and enter after the icon clears, while inactive icons settle at 60% opacity. Press-scale is deliberately absent. Hidden labels remain available through tooltips and accessibility names. Open-page tabs never shrink or wrap: the cluster measures the lane between page title and instruments, scrolls on the horizontal axis only with directional edge masks, keeps the active tab visible, and exposes every open page through a +N menu counting fully offscreen tabs. Vertical overflow is locked even when selection shadows extend beyond a tab’s box, so the 36px capsule cannot drift by a pixel. The count tracks the live scroll-lane width throughout instrument expansion and collapse, never the previous settled layout. The mask affects only the scroll lane—never the +N control—and uses transparency rather than a paper-colored overlay. Active page tabs and peek relationship tabs reuse the instrument bar’s selected-control surface and shadow as one shared moving indicator. The indicator translates and resizes beneath stable labels in 220ms; individual tabs change only content color, so selected backgrounds never crossfade or double. The page-tab scrollport reserves a 4px bleed so that shared shadow never terminates at an overflow edge. Every page tab reserves a stable close slot: × stays visible on the active tab, appears on hover or focus for inactive tabs, and Delete closes the focused tab. Its 18px circular hover surface is inset 5px inside the 28px tab, making it concentric with the pill’s end cap. Active page is one state: tab, breadcrumb, page content, and Files selection update atomically. Pointer switching enters horizontally toward the selected tab; keyboard closing skips motion. Closing the active tab selects its nearest neighbor. Closing the final tab produces a quiet “No page open” workspace, hides page instruments, and clears Files selection; choosing a Files row recreates and activates its tab. Model and reasoning are separate controls: model selection uses one searchable, provider-grouped combobox; reasoning is an adjacent single-choice menu. In settings they occupy invariant 180px + 88px columns, so labels never resize the row; the composer version stays content-sized. Reasoning never occupies the model results, and neither control nests a submenu. Both popovers grow from their own trigger and close after selection. Pressable tabs and instruments compress to .98 over 120ms; the smaller tab-close target uses .97. Hover feedback exists only for fine pointers. A tier-3 diff is a focus-plane switch: the room, desk, and any open tier-2 peek recede, dim, and defocus together in 180ms; the sheet keeps its independent entrance spring. The peek stays logically open and resumes unchanged when the diff closes; opening the diff never performs a separate peek action. The instrument ornament compacts into a 36px launcher: it stays expanded while the inner content lane has at least 520px of room and compacts automatically below that threshold. A manual expansion is temporary and survives only until the next resize. Geometry commits immediately; a separate right-anchored, layout-isolated surface performs the 260ms width morph. Its radius and shadow remain physically stable instead of being distorted by scale. Content follows the same curve through a matching right-anchored clip; opacity drops only after the clip closes. The chevron becomes an ellipsis. Tab-lane geometry never animates. Compacting never closes or changes the peek. Executable motion values live in board-motion.js; static color, type, spacing, container, breakpoint, and radius tokens live in the NTRP Desktop Paper file. TIER 4 · NOTIFICATIONS · desk material, status WORDS; newest lands bottom-right on RISE_IN, older recede up (dim + scale — the stack grammar), max 3, self-dismiss on a crisp tween. A notification center is deliberately absent — asks live on Home. Implementation: sonner, restyled to these tokens (its icons replaced by the status word; keep pushToast as the app-facing API)

Gradual blur is the boundary material (the fog-of-war thesis, design-language.md): attention has soft edges. Scroll edges defocus progressively instead of clipping; out-of-attention layers defocus — reading mode dims + blurs the furniture 2px, and the room under a blocking sheet dims, scales .98 and defocuses 2px. Blur is gradual in space (a ramped field — never a frosted pane; panels are not glass) and in time (ramps ≥150ms, never snaps or arrives pre-applied).

Engineering law (bitten twice): commit the resting state inline before animate(); completion callbacks are rAF-driven and freeze in hidden tabs. Reduced motion: cross-fade, no scale, no travel; retain short color and opacity feedback.
